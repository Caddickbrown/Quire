The long way round the reservoir
Tuesday, 8 September · 08:12 · @admin @morning
Left before seven and took the long way round the reservoir, which adds twenty minutes and, this morning, was worth every one of them.
What the light was doing
Low and flat off the water, the kind that makes the far bank look printed rather than grown. Two swans, unbothered. A man with a thermos who nodded like we had an arrangement.
The dull entries are the ones worth having in five years.
