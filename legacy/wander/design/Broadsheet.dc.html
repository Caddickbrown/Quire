The Long Way Round
the Reservoir
Twenty Minutes Longer, and Worth Every One
Left before seven and took the long way round the reservoir, which adds twenty minutes and, this morning, was worth every one of them. Low and flat off the water, the kind that makes the far bank look printed rather than grown. Two swans, unbothered. A man with a thermos who nodded like we had an arrangement. Wrote a note at breakfast out of habit and felt briefly absurd. Not everything is a changelog. Things to carry into tomorrow: finish the Hexham letter; ask about the boiler service; read twenty pages, no phone.
